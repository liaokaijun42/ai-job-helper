# AI Job Analysis Workflow

## Step 1 — Input Job Description

The user pastes a job description into the system.

---

## Step 2 — Extract Key Information

The AI extracts:

- Required skills
- Education requirements
- Years of experience
- Preferred background
- Location
- Visa or work authorization requirements
- Salary clues
- Technical keywords

---

## Step 3 — Candidate Matching

The AI compares the job requirements with the candidate profile.

Matching categories:

- Strong match
- Partial match
- Missing skills
- Competitive advantages

---

## Step 4 — Risk Assessment

The AI evaluates:

- Visa sponsorship probability
- Salary competitiveness
- Industry growth potential
- Career progression opportunities
- Technical barriers

---

## Step 5 — Resume Optimization

The AI generates:

- Resume keywords
- Suggested project descriptions
- Technical wording improvements
- ATS-friendly optimization

---

## Step 6 — Interview Preparation

The AI generates:

- Likely interview questions
- Technical preparation points
- Behavioral interview suggestions
- Company-specific preparation advice

---

## Step 7 — Final Output

The AI produces:

- Overall match score
- Key strengths
- Key weaknesses
- Action plan
- Suggested next steps
